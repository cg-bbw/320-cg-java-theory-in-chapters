== Check Your Understanding

. Why is `Car` to `Engine` modeled as composition in this example?
Because the Car creates and owns its Engine. The engine is created inside the constructor of the Car and is considered part of the car.
. Why is `Team` to `Player` modeled as aggregation?
Because a Player can exist independently of a Team. The team only stores references to existing players and does not create or control their lifetime.
. Does Java enforce the difference between aggregation and composition?
No. Java has no specific keywords for aggregation or composition. The difference depends on the intended ownership and lifetime of the objects.
. What does unidirectional mean?
Unidirectional means that only one object stores or uses a reference to the other object. The relationship only works in one direction.
. What does multiplicity describe?
Multiplicity describes how many objects can participate at one end of a relationship.

== Inspect the Code

For each relationship below, identify which class stores the reference:

. `Car` and `Engine`;
. `Team` and `Player`.

Explain why the relationships are unidirectional in the current code.

Car and Engine
- The Car class stores the reference to the Engine.
- The Engine does not store a reference to the Car.

Team and Player
- The Team class stores references to multiple Player objects in a List<Player>.
- The Player class does not store a reference to the Team.
Why are the relationships unidirectional?

Because only one side stores a reference to the other. The Car knows its Engine, and the Team knows its Players, but neither Engine nor Player knows the other side.

== Classify the Relationship

For each situation, choose the most suitable modeling term and explain your reasoning:

. A `House` creates and owns its `Room` objects.
Composition
The house creates and owns its rooms. The rooms are considered parts of the house, and the house controls their lifetime in the model.
. A `Playlist` stores references to existing `Song` objects.
Aggregation
Songs can exist independently of a playlist. The playlist only stores references to existing songs.
. A `Doctor` treats a `Patient` during an appointment.
Association
The doctor and patient interact with each other, but neither owns the other. This is a general relationship between objects.
